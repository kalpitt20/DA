# What is LAN,MAN,WAN??

LAN, MAN, and WAN — these are types of computer networks, based on how large the area they cover is.

# 🔹 1. LAN (Local Area Network)


LAN = Small area network

👉 Covers a small area like:
Home
School
Office

Examples:
Your home WiFi
College computer lab

Features:
Fast speed
Low cost
Limited area
# 🔹 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

MAN = City-level network

👉 Covers:
A whole city or large campus

Examples:
Internet service across a city
Cable TV network

Features:
Bigger than LAN
Medium speed
Connects multiple LANs

# 🔹 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)

WAN = Large/global network

👉 Covers:
Countries or whole world

Examples:

The Internet
Bank networks

* Features:

Very large area
Slower than LAN
Expensive & complex

🔥 Simple Comparison
| Type	| Full Form	| Area	| Example |
|--------|---------|--------|----------|
| LAN	| Local Area Network |	Small |	Home WiFi|
| MAN	| Metropolitan Area Network |	City	| ISP network|
| WAN	| Wide Area Network |	Global |	Internet|

💡 Easy Way to Remember
LAN → Local (small)
MAN → Metro (city)
WAN → Wide (world)
