# 🔹 What is SEO?

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) means improving your website so it appears higher in search results on search engines like Google.

👉 Simple:
SEO = Getting free traffic from Google


🔹 How SEO Works

You create a website or content
Google crawls (reads) your site
It indexes (stores) your pages
It ranks them in search results

Better SEO = Higher ranking


🔹 Types of SEO

1. On-Page SEO
Keywords in content
Proper headings (H1, H2)
Good titles & descriptions

👉 Example: Writing “Best budget phones in India” article properly

2. Off-Page SEO
Getting backlinks (other websites linking to you)
Social sharing

👉 Example: Your blog link shared on other sites

3. Technical SEO
Website speed
Mobile-friendly design
Proper structure


🔹 Why SEO is Important

✅ More website visitors (free traffic)
✅ Helps in business growth
✅ Builds trust and visibility
